# Pricing — Praxura

Last updated: 2026-11-27
Canonical pricing page: https://praxura.de/#preise
Currency: EUR (net prices; currently no VAT charged under German small business regulation § 19 UStG; if that rule no longer applies, statutory VAT is added).
Target audience: B2B only — German Heilmittel practices (Podologie, Physiotherapie, Ergotherapie, Logopädie).

## Billing Model
- Fixed monthly licence fee by number of treating therapists (Behandler:innen).
- Reception and administration staff are always included at no extra cost.
- All features are included in every tier — no feature gating, no revenue share, no commission (0 % Provision on § 302 SGB V billing).
- Server costs are paid directly by the practice to its German hosting provider (own private practice cloud, "eigene Praxis-Cloud"), or Praxura can run on a Windows 11 PC / Linux server inside the practice.

## Tiers

### Solo
- Target: 1 treating therapist (1 Behandler:in)
- Monthly licence: 59 € / month (net)
- Annual payment discount: -10 % on the licence fee
- One-time setup package: 290 € (net)
- Included: all Praxura features (scheduling, digital patient file, local Muster 13 barcode check, commission-free § 302 SGB V DTA preparation, electronic trip log, co-payment & dunning, employee management)

### Praxis
- Target: 2–5 treating therapists (2–5 Behandler:innen)
- Monthly licence: 149 € / month (net)
- Annual payment discount: -10 % on the licence fee
- One-time setup package: 490 € (net)
- Included: all Praxura features, reception/admin users included

### Team
- Target: 6–10 treating therapists (6–10 Behandler:innen)
- Monthly licence: 249 € / month (net)
- Annual payment discount: -10 % on the licence fee
- One-time setup package: 790 € (net)
- Included: all Praxura features, reception/admin users included

### Groß / Verbund
- Target: 11+ treating therapists or multiple practice locations
- Monthly licence: Custom quote — contact kontakt@praxura.de
- One-time setup package: Custom quote

## What the One-Time Setup Package Includes
- Setup of the practice's own private cloud server in the practice's own account with a German hosting provider (or local Windows 11 / Linux installation)
- Configuration of master data and services (Stammdaten & Leistungen)
- Standard data migration (patients and open prescriptions)
- Encrypted automated backups and update setup
- 2 online onboarding trainings and full access handover

## Contract Terms & Guarantee
- Minimum term: 12 months, then cancellable monthly.
- Money-back guarantee: 60-day money-back guarantee on the monthly licence fee (excludes setup package, hosting/server costs, and separately commissioned services; once per practice).
- Free consultation: 30-minute live video consultation (Analysegespräch) bookable at https://praxura.de/demo-booking.html

## Not Included / Not Currently Offered
- Telematikinfrastruktur (TI) connection: not currently included (optional add-on via a certified TI provider planned; no fixed release date or price yet).
- AI / OCR prescription scanning: not currently active.
- External billing center service (Abrechnungszentrum): Praxura is software for commission-free § 302 preparation; the practice signs and submits with its own ITSG certificate.
